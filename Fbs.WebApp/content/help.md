## How can I view more information about an event?

Double-click any event on the calendar to see its full details, notes, and who booked it.

---

## How can I make a new booking?

Click or tap where you want your booking to start, then drag down to where it ends. On a phone, press and hold for half a second before dragging so the page doesn't scroll instead. Want a guided tour? Click **Show me around** at the top of the New booking page.

---

## How can I adjust the timeline view on mobile?

Drag the bottom-left corner of the timeline view to pan or zoom across dates.

---

## How can I adjust the start and end time of a booking?

Drag the bottom edge of your selected time block up or down before confirming.

---

## How can I book a facility for the whole day?

Select any slot on the facility, then choose **All day**. The booking runs from midnight to midnight.

---

## How can I book several facilities or days at once?

Click **Book multiple**, choose your facilities and dates, and set the hours. You can also pick slots one by one on the timeline and click **Add to list**.

---

## How do multi-day bookings work?

By default, BookASpace creates a separate booking for each day. Turn off **Split multi-day bookings** to book consecutive days as one continuous reservation instead.

---

## What happens if one of my slots clashes?

None of the slots go through. Selected slots are booked together as a single batch, so remove any overlapping slot and try again.

---

## What is the difference between Eiger and Temasek Square?

Eiger is the outer running track around the parade square. Temasek Square is the inner parade square area itself.

---

## How can I see all upcoming bookings?

Open **Timeline** from the sidebar. Each facility has its own row, with a red line marking the current time. Filter by facility or click a facility name to focus on what you need.
